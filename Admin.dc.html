Why these totals carry no breakdown

Everything here is summed across the shops, never split between them — including the money. A per-shop figure would answer "which shops are driving this?" — and the honest answer is that the question is the first half of a promotion tool. The appetite arrives before the feature does, so the number is not stored in a form this surface can query.
