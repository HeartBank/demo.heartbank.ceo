Why there is no colour picker here

Two values in every theme cannot be derived from the accent: the ink that sits on the fill, and the soft variant on a light ground. Fire Opal fails at 3.40 where Sapphire passes at 7.00 — and no formula picks correctly between them.

So the shape is

A gem, plus two measured overrides, checked on both grounds before it is ever offered. Adding a seventh theme is a measurement, not a setting — which is why it happens here and not in a shop's own settings.
